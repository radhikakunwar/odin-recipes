Aloo Paratha

Description

Aloo Paratha is a popular Indian flatbread stuffed with a delicious spiced potato filling. It is made with wheat flour and mashed potatoes mixed with flavorful herbs and spices. The paratha is cooked on a hot tawa until golden brown and crispy on the outside. It is commonly served hot with curd, pickle, butter, or chutney.

Ingredients

  • Wheat flour
  • Boiled potatoes
  • Green chillies
  • Fresh coriander leaves
  • Red chilli powder
  • Garam masala
  • Amchur powder
  • Carom seeds (ajwain)
  • Salt
  • Oil or ghee
  • Water

Steps

  • Boil the potatoes, peel them, and mash them well.
  • Add green chillies, coriander leaves, red chilli powder, garam masala, amchur powder, ajwain, and salt to the mashed potatoes.
  • Mix everything well to prepare the potato filling.
  • Prepare a soft dough using wheat flour, water, and a little oil.
  • Divide the dough into small balls and roll each one into a small circle.
  • Place the potato filling in the center, seal the edges, and gently roll it into a paratha.
  • Cook the paratha on a hot tawa until both sides become golden brown.
  • Apply some ghee or butter and serve hot with curd, pickle, or chutney.
