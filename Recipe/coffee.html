Cold Coffee

Description

Cold coffee is a refreshing and creamy beverage made with chilled milk, coffee, and sugar. It has a rich coffee flavor with a smooth and frothy texture. It can be blended with ice to make it perfectly chilled and refreshing. Cold coffee is often topped with whipped cream or chocolate syrup for extra flavor. It is a perfect drink to enjoy on a warm day or as a delicious afternoon treat.

Ingredients

  • Chilled milk
  • Instant coffee
  • Sugar
  • Ice cubes
  • Vanilla ice cream
  • Chocolate syrup
  • Whipped cream

Steps

  • Add chilled milk, instant coffee, sugar, and ice cubes to a blender.
  • Blend everything until the mixture becomes smooth and frothy.
  • Add a scoop of vanilla ice cream and blend again for a creamy texture.
  • Drizzle some chocolate syrup inside a serving glass.
  • Pour the cold coffee into the glass.
  • Top it with whipped cream and a little chocolate syrup.
  • Serve immediately and enjoy your refreshing cold coffee!
