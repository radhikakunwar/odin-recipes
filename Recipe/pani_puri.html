Pani Puri

Discription

Pani Puri is a popular Indian street food made with crispy hollow puris filled with spicy, tangy water, potatoes, chickpeas, and flavorful chutneys. It is loved for its delicious combination of crunchy, spicy, and refreshing flavors.

Ingredients

🥣 For the Pani

  • Mint leaves
  • Coriander leaves
  • Green chillies
  • Tamarind
  • Lemon juice
  • Roasted cumin powder
  • Black salt
  • Chaat masala
  • Salt
  • Water

🥔 For the Filling

  • Boiled chickpeas
  • Boiled potatoes
  • Onion (optional)
  • Chaat masala
  • Red chilli powder
  • Salt

Steps

  • Prepare the spicy and tangy pani using mint, coriander, green chilli, tamarind, and spices.
  • Boil and mash the potatoes, then mix them with boiled chickpeas and spices.
  • Make a small hole in each crispy puri.
  • Fill each puri with the potato and chickpea mixture.
  • Add some sweet chutney and spicy pani to each puri.
  • Serve immediately and enjoy your delicious pani puri!
