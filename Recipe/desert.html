Strawberry Roll

Description

Strawberry Roll is a delicious and delightful dessert made with a soft, fluffy cake and a creamy strawberry filling.The cake is carefully rolled to create a beautiful spiral shape and is filled with fresh, sweet strawberry cream.It has a perfect combination of fruity, creamy, and sweet flavors. The soft texture of the cake makes every bite light and enjoyable.Strawberry Roll is a wonderful dessert to serve at parties, celebrations, or simply enjoy as a sweet treat.

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Eggs
  • Sugar
  • Vanilla extract
  • Fresh strawberries
  • Whipped cream
  • Milk
  • Butter
  • Baking powder
  • Powdered sugar

Steps

  • Preheat the oven and prepare a baking tray with parchment paper.
  • Mix eggs and sugar until the mixture becomes light and fluffy.
  • Add flour, baking powder, vanilla extract, and milk to make a smooth cake batter.
  • Spread the batter evenly on the baking tray and bake until the cake is soft and lightly golden.
  • Allow the cake to cool slightly, then spread whipped cream and chopped strawberries over it.
  • Carefully roll the cake from one end to the other to form a log shape.
  • Dust the Strawberry Roll with powdered sugar and garnish with fresh strawberries.
  • Refrigerate for a while, slice, and serve chilled.
